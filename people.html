Prof. Pranab K. Muhuri

Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering & Principal Investigator, CI Lab

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Technology, South Asian University, New Delhi, India

Full Profile & Publications

Prof. Pranab K. Muhuri leads the Computational Intelligence Lab (CI Lab) at South Asian University. His research interests include computational intelligence, fuzzy systems, real-time systems, evolutionary optimization, perceptual computing and transfer learning.

RESEARCH SPECIALIZATIONS

Computational Intelligence, Type-2 Fuzzy Sets and Systems, Real-Time Embedded Systems, Evolutionary Optimization, Perceptual Computing and Transfer Learning.
